Supervised Undergraduate Projects

2016

Comprehensive List of Matrices, Symbols, and Curvatures

I supervised 3 undergraduate students on this project.

The project focused on organizing key concepts and notation related to matrices and curvature, highlighting their roles in mathematical descriptions of geometry.

2014

Exact Solutions for the Age of the Universe

I supervised 3 undergraduate students on this project.

The project explored mathematical approaches to the age of the universe, with a focus on exact solutions and their interpretation in cosmological models.

2013

Some Applications of Laplace and Fourier Series

I supervised 2 undergraduate students on this project.

The project examined applications of Laplace methods and Fourier series to mathematical problems, emphasizing how transform and series representations support analysis.
