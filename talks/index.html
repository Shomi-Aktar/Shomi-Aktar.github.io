Selected Presentations

2026 Contributed Talk

Memory-Driven First Passage Time Analysis of Deformable Fiber Transport in Perforated Media

SIAM Annual Meeting (AN26) · CP17: Stochastic Systems

Presented research on first-passage dynamics and memory-dependent transport of deformable fibers in geometrically heterogeneous perforated media. Joint work with Mojdeh Rasoulzadeh.

2026 Poster

Semi-Analytical Modeling of Shifted Generalized-Gamma Waiting-Time Distributions in Random Porous Media

SIAM Annual Meeting (AN26) · Association for Women in Mathematics

Poster presentation on semi-analytical descriptions of waiting-time distributions and anomalous transport in random porous environments. Joint work with Mojdeh Rasoulzadeh.

2026 Contributed Talk

Anomalous Transport of Deformable Fibers in Microstructured Media

AMS Spring Southeastern Sectional Meeting

Presented a semi-analytical CTRW-based framework for describing anomalous transport of deformable fibers in microstructured environments.

2025 Contributed Talk

Anomalous Transport of Microplastic Fibers in Porous Media

APS Division of Fluid Dynamics Annual Meeting

Presented research examining transport and anomalous breakthrough behavior of elongated microplastic fibers through heterogeneous porous media.

2025 Invited Talk

Anomalous Transport of Deformable Particles (Filaments) Around Circular Obstacles

Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM)

Invited presentation on mathematical modeling of deformable filament transport and the influence of circular obstacles on particle migration.

2025 Poster

Diffusion of Elongated and Deformable Particles in Perforated Media

Third Joint Alabama–Florida Conference on Differential Equations, Dynamical Systems and Applications

Presented research on diffusion and transport of elongated particles in geometrically heterogeneous perforated domains.

2025 3-Minute Research Presentation

Research Communication in Hydrologic and Environmental Transport

CIROH @ UA · 3-Minute Thesis Competition

Presented research related to hydrologic and environmental transport processes in a concise three-minute research communication format.
