Current Research Directions

Microfiber Transport

Mathematical and computational investigation of elongated microplastic transport through heterogeneous porous environments.

Memory & Breakthrough Dynamics

Stochastic and memory-dependent descriptions of non-Fickian transport, residence times, and breakthrough behavior.

First-Passage Problems

Semi-analytical analysis of first-passage and diffusion problems in domains containing multiple geometrical obstacles.
