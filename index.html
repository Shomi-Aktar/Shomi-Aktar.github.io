About

Mathematics across scales

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at The University of Alabama. My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, stochastic processes, transport phenomena, and scientific computing.

My work focuses on the transport of elongated and deformable particles through heterogeneous porous structures. I combine pore-scale computational modeling with stochastic upscaling, memory-dependent transport equations, first-passage-time analysis, and semi-analytical methods.

A central goal of my research is to understand how microscopic particle dynamics, pore geometry, and temporal memory collectively determine macroscopic breakthrough and transport behavior.
